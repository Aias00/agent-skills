# Starter Allocations

Use this file when the user is new, small-capital, or asks for a concrete starter mix before a lane is clearly chosen.

This file is a route-level summary. Once `fund-playbook.md`, `equity-playbook.md`, or `crypto-playbook.md` is loaded, the lane-specific playbook overrides this file for concrete sizing and starter buckets.

## Default assumptions

- this is learning capital, not emergency cash
- low turnover
- no leverage or derivatives
- keep the first version easy to understand and easy to follow

## Fund-first starter

### Under `1000` yuan

Default:

- `80%` broad-market index fund or ETF
- `20%` cash

### `1000-5000` yuan

Default:

- `70%` broad-market index fund or ETF
- `20%` secondary broad exposure such as global or growth index
- `10%` cash

### `5000-20000` yuan

Default:

- `60%` broad-market index fund or ETF
- `20%` global or growth exposure
- `10%` bond or cash-like sleeve
- `10%` optional satellite

## Equity-first starter

Use only when brokerage access and lot-size practicality are confirmed.

For exact Hong Kong/U.S. equity starter tiers and allocations, use `equity-playbook.md`.

### Under `3000` yuan

- prefer one broad-market ETF or `simulation-first`
- keep a visible cash buffer
- avoid single stocks

### `3000-20000` yuan

- stay ETF-first
- allow at most one quality single-stock slot
- keep a meaningful cash buffer for fee and FX drag

Rules:

- add no more than one single stock in the first version
- if board lots or FX costs distort sizing, stay ETF-first

## Crypto-first starter

Use only when the user explicitly wants crypto and legal access is verified.

### Under `2000` yuan

Default:

- `80-100% BTC`
- optional `0-20%` cash only if future adds are likely

Rules:

- under `500` yuan, prefer `100% BTC` or `simulation` if fee drag is too high
- minimize transaction count
- do not add `ETH` unless fee drag and order-size practicality support a second sleeve

### `2000-10000` yuan

Default:

- `65%` BTC
- `20%` ETH
- `15%` cash

Rules:

- keep it spot-only
- avoid on-chain activity for tiny accounts
- do not diversify into many tokens
- do not automate live trading until there is at least `30` days of paper-trade discipline

## Cross-lane core-satellite rule

If the user wants mixed exposure:

- keep `70-90%` in fund-first or ETF-first core
- cap crypto at `10-20%` of total beginner capital unless the user explicitly wants a crypto-first plan
- add single stocks only after the core is already in place
