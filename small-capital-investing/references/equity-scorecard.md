# Equity Scorecard

Use this rubric for any Hong Kong or U.S. stock or ETF scorecard.

## Factor weights

| Factor | Weight | What to check |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Growth | 25 | Revenue trend, EPS or profit trend, core segment acceleration |
| Profitability | 20 | Gross margin, operating margin, margin trend, guidance quality |
| Cash flow and capital allocation | 15 | Operating cash flow, free cash flow, CAPEX discipline, buybacks |
| Valuation | 15 | Relative to own history, peers, and expected growth |
| Catalysts | 15 | Next earnings, product, buyback, policy, or index events |
| Risk | 10 | Regulation, geopolitics, customer concentration, inventory, accounting red flags |

## Score bands

- `80-100`: `Buy-watch`
- `70-79`: `Track`
- `<70`: `Skip`

Any unresolved red flag can override the numeric score.

## Minimum evidence package

Before scoring, verify:

- latest quarter headline numbers
- management guidance or lack of guidance
- next catalyst date
- at least three bullish facts
- at least three concrete risks
- one explicit invalidation condition
